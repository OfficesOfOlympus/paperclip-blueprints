## 1. Target Industry Segments

### Primary Segment: SaaS / B2B Software (Priority 1)

Rationale: Paperclip's core product (control plane for AI-agent companies) is inherently a B2B SaaS tool. The primary ICP should be companies already operating in or adjacent to SaaS, where the language, buying process, and pain points align directly.

**Sub-verticals (ranked by fit):**
- AI/ML infrastructure tools — companies building agent frameworks, LLM tooling, or AI workflows
- Revenue operations / GTM enablement — companies in sales engagement, CRM, lead gen, or outbound
- Developer tools / infrastructure — companies serving engineering teams with API-first products
- Customer success / support automation — companies automating support workflows

### Secondary Segment: E-commerce (Priority 2 — Q1–Q2 rollout)

**Why now, not first:** E-commerce has distinct buying cycles, different decision-makers (CMO vs VP Sales), and different pain signals (seasonal hiring spikes vs. steady SaaS growth).

**Sub-verticals:**
- D2C brands doing $2M–$20M ARR (need operations tools but not enterprise complexity)
- E-commerce SaaS platforms (Shopify ecosystem tools, headless commerce)

### Tertiary Segment: Manufacturing & Industrial (Priority 3 — H2 rollout)

**Why last:** Manufacturing has longer sales cycles, fragmented tech stacks, and often requires on-premise or hybrid deployment. Paperclip is cloud-native — this is a later-play market.

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Firmographic Dimensions

| Attribute | Primary ICP Definition | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| **Company size** | 20–250 employees | Sweet spot: large enough to have budget and operational complexity, small enough that a single tool can change workflows |
| **Revenue** | $1M–$25M ARR | Startups past seed/Series A with real revenue to spend on growth tools |
| **Industry** | B2B SaaS, AI/ML tools, GTM enablement, dev tools | Keep to 3–4 verticals maximum; expand after proving product-market fit in primary segment |
| **Company stage** | Seed to Series B funded, or bootstrapped profitable ($1M+ ARR) | Venture-backed signals budget authority; profitable bootstrapped signals willingness to pay for ROI tools |
| **Geography** | North America (US + Canada), then UK/Western Europe | English-first product; regulatory simplicity (GDPR later) |

### Situational / Trigger Dimensions

**Strong buying triggers (score highest):**
- Recently raised funding (last 6 months) — especially Series A or B
- Hired a new VP Sales, Head of Revenue, or RevOps leader (last 90 days)
- Actively hiring for multiple roles (indicates scaling and operational pain)
- Expanded to a new market or launched a new product line
- Switched or added a key platform (CRM, sales engagement, analytics)

**Moderate triggers:**
- Company growth >30% YoY (from public data signals)
- Leadership transition (new CEO or CRO within 180 days)
- Press coverage indicating expansion or new investment

**Negative indicators (disqualify):**
- Companies under 12 months old (instability, no budget yet)
- Competitor companies (other agent control planes or agent platforms)
- Companies with 500+ employees (need enterprise sales motion, not cold outreach)
- Student projects, hackathon startups, pre-revenue entities

### Buying Committee

For a company of Paperclip's type (multi-agent orchestration, $10K–$100K+ ACV potential):
- **Economic buyer:** CEO, CTO, or Head of Revenue/Operations
- **Champion:** VP of Engineering, Head of AI/ML, or RevOps lead
- **Influencers:** Engineering managers, product managers, data/ML leads
- **Blockers:** Legal/Compliance (data privacy), Security (enterprise buyers)

## 5. Lead Scoring Criteria

### Scoring Model Overview

Each lead is scored 1–10 across three dimensions:

| Dimension | Max Points | Weight |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Company Fit | 0–4 | 40% |
| Contact Fit | 0–3 | 30% |
| Timing Signal | 0–3 | 30% |
| **Total** | **1–10** | **100%** |

---

### Dimension 1: Company Fit (0–4 points)

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Industry match | 0–1 | 1 = Core vertical (AI/ML, B2B SaaS, GTM tools). 0 = Adjacent or tangential. |
| Company size | 0–1 | 1 = 20–250 employees (ICP sweet spot). 0.5 = 10–19 or 251–500. 0 = Outside ICP range. |
| Revenue / funding | 0–1 | 1 = $1M–$25M ARR or Series A–B funded. 0.5 = Seed stage or <$1M ARR. 0 = Pre-revenue or 500+ employees. |
| Tech stack alignment | 0–1 | 1 = Uses complementary platforms (Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, AWS, GCP). 0.5 = Uses basic stack. 0 = Uses competing platform. |

### Dimension 2: Contact Fit (0–3 points)

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Role / seniority | 0–1.5 | 1.5 = Economic buyer (CEO, CTO, Head of Revenue, VP Engineering). 1.0 = Key influencer (Engineering Manager, RevOps lead, Head of AI). 0.5 = Mid-level. 0 = Individual contributor. |
| Contact accuracy | 0–1 | 1 = Email verified, role confirmed on LinkedIn/company site. 0.5 = Email found but not verified. 0 = No contact data. |
| Decision influence | 0–0.5 | 0.5 = Directly responsible for the pain Paperclip solves. 0 = Peripheral stakeholder. |

### Dimension 3: Timing Signal (0–3 points)

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Recent funding | 0–1 | 1 = Funded in last 6 months. 0.5 = Funded 6–12 months ago. 0 = No recent funding. |
| Hiring activity | 0–1 | 1 = Hiring 3+ roles in last 30 days (expansion signal). 0.5 = Hiring 1–2 roles. 0 = No recent hiring. |
| Product / market expansion | 0–0.5 | 0.5 = Launched new product, entered new market, or restructured. 0 = Stable. |
| Leadership change | 0–0.5 | 0.5 = New C-level or VP hire in last 90 days (they evaluate tools within 90–180 days). 0 = No change. |

---

### Score Tiers and Dispatch Rules

| Score | Tier | Action |
|-------|------|--------|
| **8–10** | Hot lead | Dispatch to outreach queue IMMEDIATELY. Highest priority. Contact within 24 hours of qualification. |
| **5–7** | Warm lead | Queue for next campaign batch. Follow up within 1–2 weeks. Add to nurture sequence if timing is off. |
| **1–4** | Cold lead | Archive. Revisit only during campaign-specific initiatives or if a new timing signal emerges. |

### Calibration Requirements

- **Monthly review:** Score top 10% of contacts and compare to average close rate. Adjust weights if hot leads don't convert.
- **Quarterly ICP audit:** Pull closed-won and closed-lost deals. Verify the ICP dimensions still predict outcomes.
- **Negative scoring:** Apply -1 point for competitor companies, -0.5 for companies under 12 months old.
