## 1. Launch Timeline

### Phase 1: Foundation (Day 1-7)

**Day 1-2: Setup & Alignment**
- [ ] CEO approves initial product focus areas and target verticals
- [ ] Marketing Manager defines campaign brief and messaging framework
- [ ] Lead Researcher receives target criteria and begins sourcing
- [ ] Content Writer begins SEO topic research based on agreed focus areas
- [ ] Outreach Specialist reviews messaging framework and prepares templates

**Day 3-4: Research & Content**
- [ ] Lead Researcher delivers first batch of 50 qualified leads with scores
- [ ] Content Writer drafts first 2 blog articles (SEO-focused)
- [ ] Outreach Specialist drafts cold email sequence v1 (2 sequences: cold intro + follow-up)
- [ ] Paid Ads Manager sets up campaign structure in Meta/LinkedIn dashboards

**Day 5-7: Review & Refine**
- [ ] CEO reviews lead quality (sample of 10), email templates, and blog drafts
- [ ] Marketing Manager spots-checks brand consistency across all content
- [ ] Lead Researcher refines scoring criteria based on CEO feedback
- [ ] Outreach Specialist finalizes email sequences after CEO approval
- [ ] Content Writer publishes first blog articles
- [ ] Team sync: review all assets, confirm Day 8 readiness

**Key Milestones (End of Day 7)**
- [ ] 50+ qualified leads in outreach queue
- [ ] 2 published blog articles
- [ ] Approved email sequences (cold intro + 3-touch follow-up)
- [ ] Ad campaign structures built and awaiting launch
- [ ] All brand assets and messaging templates finalized

---

### Phase 2: Soft Launch (Week 2, Days 8-14)

**Objectives**: Test messaging, validate lead quality, calibrate delivery rates

- [ ] **Day 8**: Launch first wave — 20 emails via Outreach Specialist (small batch to test deliverability)
- [ ] **Day 8**: Twitter/X Outreach begins engagement (15 prospects)
- [ ] **Day 8**: LinkedIn Outreach sends connection requests (20 prospects)
- [ ] **Day 9**: Monitor email open rates, delivery, and spam complaints
- [ ] **Day 10**: Content Writer drafts second batch of 2 blog articles
- [ ] **Day 10**: Reply Handler begins processing first inbound replies
- [ ] **Day 12**: Weekly review — analyze opens, clicks, replies, spam rate
- [ ] **Day 13**: Adjust targeting if open rate < 25% or spam complaints > 0.1%
- [ ] **Day 14**: Expand to 50 emails/day if metrics are healthy

**Soft Launch KPIs (Thresholds)**
- Email open rate: target 25%+
- Click rate: target 3%+
- Reply rate: target 5%+
- Spam complaint rate: target < 0.1%
- Leads/day from research: target 10+

---

### Phase 3: Full Launch (Week 3-4, Days 15-28)

**Objectives**: Scale outreach, optimize conversion, build pipeline

- [ ] **Day 15**: Scale to full volume — 100 emails/day across all channels
- [ ] **Day 15**: Launch paid ad campaigns (Meta + LinkedIn) with approved creatives
- [ ] **Day 15**: Ad Creative delivers 3-5 ad variants per platform
- [ ] **Day 16-17**: Content Writer publishes 2 more blog articles (total 6 by end of Week 3)
- [ ] **Day 18**: Scaling Manager evaluates which verticals perform best
- [ ] **Day 20**: International Research shares arbitrage opportunities for targeting expansion
- [ ] **Day 21**: Full-week review — pipeline health, booked calls, cost per lead
- [ ] **Day 22-24**: A/B test subject lines and CTAs on underperforming sequences
- [ ] **Day 25**: Scaling Manager presents vertical performance report
- [ ] **Day 26-28**: Shift budget/resources to top 2 performing verticals
- [ ] **Day 28**: Complete 4-week performance report for CEO

**Full Launch KPIs**
- Qualified leads/week: 30+
- Response rate: 5%+
- Call bookings/week: 5+
- Pipeline stage completion rate: 90%+
- Content articles published/week: 2+
- Cost per lead (paid ads): target $15-25

## 6. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|-------|
| Email deliverability issues (spam folder) | Medium | High | Warm domain, start with 20-email test batch, monitor spam complaints daily | Outreach Specialist |
| Lead quality below target | Medium | High | CEO review on Day 5 sample; refine scoring criteria immediately if needed | Lead Researcher |
| Slow response rate (< 3%) | Medium | Medium | A/B test subject lines by Day 10; adjust targeting by Day 12 | Outreach Specialist |
| Content production bottleneck | Low | Medium | Batch-write 2 articles per cycle; maintain editorial calendar | Content Writer |
| API/integration failure | Low | High | Backup communication channel; daily health checks on all APIs | Marketing Manager |
| Budget overrun (paid ads) | Medium | Medium | Set daily spend caps; daily budget monitoring; auto-pause at 110% of daily budget | Paid Ads Manager |
| Lead duplication across channels | High | Low | Shared lead dedup database; unique lead ID tracking | Lead Researcher |
| Negative brand sentiment | Low | High | Reply Handler flags negative replies immediately; Marketing Manager escalation protocol | Reply Handler |
| Scaling Manager confusion (cross-vertical) | Low | Medium | Clear vertical ownership assignment in Phase 2 | Scaling Manager |
| International research misalignment | Low | Medium | International Research coordinates with Lead Researcher weekly | International Research |

### Escalation Protocol

| Severity | Trigger | Response | Escalation Path |
|----------|---------|----------|-----------------|
| **Critical** | API down, domain blocked, budget >150% | Immediate stop + notify | Marketing Manager → CEO (within 1 hour) |
| **High** | Spam rate >0.5%, response <2% | Pause channel, investigate | Marketing Manager resolves within 24 hours |
| **Medium** | Lead quality issues, content delay | Adjust criteria/process | Marketing Manager resolves within 48 hours |
| **Low** | Minor process improvement needed | Log and address in weekly review | Agent self-resolves or flags to Marketing Manager |
